# PhD Outreach Email Project

## Purpose

This repository is used to prepare one carefully researched PhD outreach email at a time for Shanshan Hao.

Before working on a case, read these files in order:

1. `WORKFLOW.md`
2. `references/candidate_profile.md`
3. The relevant file under `cases/`
4. Any CV, paper, transcript, or supporting material named in that case file

## Required behavior

- Treat every professor as a separate research task. Never produce a generic bulk-email template.
- Research the professor before drafting. Use current official university pages and recent original research papers. Prefer work from the last three to five years.
- Do not rely on paper titles or profile keywords. Understand the biological question, experimental system, central result, proposed mechanism, limitation, and genuine connection to the candidate.
- Focus the email on one recent original paper that creates the most natural connection. Mention a wider research theme only when useful.
- Carry out all three writing passes in `WORKFLOW.md`. The final email must reflect the third pass, not the first draft.
- Use short, natural sentences. Keep the tone academic but human. Write from the position of a trained student who wants to learn, not a mature principal investigator.
- Include one or at most two specific research questions or observations. They must arise from the professor's actual work and the candidate's real experience. Do not ask a question already answered by the paper.
- Respect every scientific boundary in `references/candidate_profile.md`. Never strengthen an association into an established causal mechanism.
- Do not invent results, techniques, publications, attachments, awards, interests, or familiarity with a professor.
- Do not claim that two projects are identical. Use cautious language such as "This raised a related question for me" when the relationship is conceptual.
- Do not use flattering or formulaic language such as "perfectly aligns," "groundbreaking," "renowned," "esteemed," "cutting-edge," "deeply impressed," or "ideal platform."
- Do not mention AI, drafting passes, prompts, or research notes in the email.

## Missing information

Do not draft the final email until the case contains:

- professor's full name;
- target university and programme or department;
- official profile URL or another reliable identity reference;
- intended intake year;
- enough candidate material to verify any case-specific claim.

If a detail is missing or two people may have the same name, stop and ask a short clarification question. Never guess.

## Deliverables

Create two files for each completed case:

- `outputs/<case-slug>_research_notes.md`
- `outputs/<case-slug>_email.md`

Research notes must contain the source links, access date, paper selection rationale, verified findings, limitation, and the proposed connection. The email file must contain only the copy-ready subject line, English email, and Chinese translation.

Before finishing, run the checklist at the end of `WORKFLOW.md`.
