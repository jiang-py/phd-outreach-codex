- TTF inhibits homologous recombination;
- RAD51AP1 is the essential target of TTF;
- the evidence establishes a definite linear chain of ROS to RAD51AP1 to homologous-recombination inhibition to cell death.

- changes associated with oxidative stress, DNA damage and RAD51AP1;
